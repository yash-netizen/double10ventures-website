At a restaurant built around its interiors and tableware, what goes on the table matters as much as the operation behind it. We supported Bhaijaan on both fronts: how the restaurant runs, and the tableware it serves on.

01

Hospitality consulting

Anmoll Nackra brought 29 years of food and beverage experience to the opening, with pre-opening support and operational guidance on the practical requirements of establishing and running a premium Indian restaurant.

02

Tableware sourcing & international supply

We sourced the tableware showcased throughout the restaurant from India, from serving bowls and platters to crockery, and coordinated its supply to New York. The aim was the right products, finishes and presentation styles for Bhaijaan's dining concept, practical enough for everyday restaurant service.

What this project showsHospitality expertise and international sourcing under one roof: understanding what a restaurant needs, then finding and arranging the right materials from India for a business overseas.
